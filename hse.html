Health, Safety & Environment

Safety is not a priority. It is a value.

Most human activities carry special risk. Our Work Code & Safety Assurance programme sets out the policies, procedures and responsibilities that keep every person on our sites safe, because a job is never so urgent that it cannot be done safely.

Every new employee receives an orientation briefing on site hazards, PPE, emergency procedures and their responsibilities before work begins.

  • 01All accidents and injuries can be prevented.
  • 02Safety must never be sacrificed for expediency.
  • 03Supervisors are accountable for the safety of every worker under their control.
  • 04Safety representatives inspect, investigate and report on all incidents and near misses.
  • 05Toolbox talks, PPE and permit-to-work systems on every job.
Our Safety Programme

Clear duties at every level.

Our Work Code & Safety Assurance manual is the baseline for every project, and is reviewed and amended whenever necessary to reflect changing standards.

Safety Representatives

  • Implement the safety programme approved by the company
  • Manage day-to-day safety activities
  • Investigate and report all incidents
  • Prepare, distribute and monitor toolbox meetings
  • Organise safety inspections and training

Supervisors

  • Responsible for the safety of every worker under their control
  • Ensure safety regulations are followed on site
  • Enforce rules and keep proper housekeeping
  • Hold toolbox meetings with their crews
  • Ensure correct personal protective equipment is used

Employee Orientation

  • Scope and nature of the project
  • Responsibilities of each employee
  • Personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • Emergency procedures and reporting near misses
  • Handling of tools, scaffolds, ladders and confined spaces
