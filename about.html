Our Company

A trusted partner to operators across onshore and offshore assets.

Ulanzi Global Limited is an indigenous Nigerian energy services company providing construction, operations and maintenance, subsea engineering, marine, and petroleum product supply services to the oil and gas industry.

We have consistently retained long-term operations and maintenance contracts with operators for both onshore and offshore assets. Our highly trained personnel deliver platform and plant operations and planned maintenance programmes efficiently and safely, in close proximity to live processing plants, without disrupting production.

Our Vision

To earn ever more trust from existing and potential clients through alternative, unique methods of dealing, managing and delivering our services.

Our Focus

A differentiated client experience, with petroleum products and services available on demand, with flawless, seamless delivery.
